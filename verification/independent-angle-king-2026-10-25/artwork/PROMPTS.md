## bots

Create ONE production-ready game sprite atlas for an original Korean elementary mathematics turn-based aiming game called Angle King. A new original character family: friendly geometric kite/triangular robots, NOT worms, NOT animals, no humanoid soldier heads, no military helmets, no resemblance to existing game mascots. Each robot has a rounded triangular turquoise chassis, ONE round amber camera eye, a tiny segmented antenna, two short boot-like mechanical feet, and two small articulated arms. Upbeat expressive personality, tactile painted 2D cartoon game art with crisp dark outlines, warm highlights, readable when reduced to 44 pixels high. EXACTLY TEN full-body robots in a perfectly regular FIVE-COLUMN by TWO-ROW sprite atlas, ONE character centered in EACH equally sized rectangular cell. Every robot faces RIGHT in the same neutral standing pose, same scale, fully visible, ample transparent gutters, no overlap. The ten variants in reading order differ by accent panels and antenna/head attachments: 1 turquoise scout with orange cheek panels, 2 coral prism bot with tiny triangular top fin, 3 mint orbit bot with one small satellite antenna, 4 bronze maker bot with small square tool pouch, 5 yellow spark bot with zigzag antenna, 6 violet relay bot with two short antennae, 7 blue comet bot with short swept fin, 8 ice-blue polar bot with soft white edge panels, 9 gold captain bot with three little prismatic top fins (not a crown with jewels), 10 white-and-orange nova bot with round side panels. All share the same distinct triangular single-camera-eye robot anatomy; no hands or faces resembling worms. TRANSPARENT BACKGROUND, no opaque panels, no text, no numbers, no labels, no cell outlines, no ground shadows beyond tiny contact shading, no weapons, no other objects. Wide landscape atlas, ideally 2500x1000, exact 5x2 equal-cell layout. This file will be sliced into ten cells by the renderer, so exact cell count and placement are critical.

## items

Create ONE production-ready transparent item sprite atlas for an original colorful 2D turn-based robot aiming game for elementary mathematics. EXACTLY TEN isolated item icons in a perfectly regular FIVE-COLUMN by TWO-ROW layout, one centered object per equal cell. Crisp dark outlines, tactile hand-painted cartoon shading, bright turquoise orange violet gold palette, very legible as 36-pixel icons. Each object fully fits its cell with generous transparent gutters, no overlap. The exact objects in reading order are: 1 a compact turquoise wind cannon shaped like a rounded rectangular electric blower with a broad circular nozzle and tiny orange turbine; 2 a round purple bouncing capsule with a cyan equatorial band and small elastic bumper feet, NO military grenade shape; 3 a faceted gold STARLIGHT CORE, crystalline star inside a turquoise hexagonal cage, NO cross, halo, wings or religious symbols; 4 a magnetic wire launcher, compact square orange spool with a flexible teal cable ending in a horseshoe magnet, NO grappling hook; 5 a tiny cheerful wind-up two-wheel rover cart with a clockwork key, single amber robot camera and folded antenna, NO sheep or animal and NO strapped explosives; 6 a turquoise TIME CUBE with a simple white clock face and orange corner bumpers, NO dynamite sticks or fuse; 7 a low cyan hexagonal SENSOR TILE with an amber central lens and three tiny raised sensor nubs, NO red pushbutton military mine; 8 a purple telescoping IMPACT MITTEN on an orange spring mechanism, rounded robotic glove, NO human severed hand or rocket; 9 a three-prong white-and-orange COORDINATE BEACON projecting a small cyan holographic diamond, NO portal gun; 10 a compact white-and-orange twin-fin flight backpack with small turquoise side thrusters, no military jetpack resemblance. Wide landscape atlas ideally 2500x1000; exactly 5 equal columns and 2 equal rows so code can address each cell reliably.
